The 6-Month Curriculum Roadmap

Each month focuses on a core theme designed to equip individuals, youth, and families with spiritual anchors, emotional wisdom, and practical habits.

March • Month 1

Reclaiming Your Story

Identity & Overcoming Stigma

Breaking the silence of trauma and mental health battles. Discovering that a diagnosis or past failure does not define your future.

"For I know the plans I have for you... plans to give you hope and a future." — Jeremiah 29:11
April • Month 2

Resilience Through the Seasons

Faith in Hardship

How to keep your spiritual and emotional anchor steady when external circumstances, health, or environments shift drastically.

"The Lord will guide you always; he will satisfy your needs in a sun-scorched land." — Isaiah 58:11
May • Month 3

The Power of Creative Renewal

Art, Music & Outlets

Harnessing visual art, song creation, and quiet writing as divine tools for processing deep trauma and finding internal balance.

"He has put a new song in my mouth, a hymn of praise to our God." — Psalm 40:3
June • Month 4

Discipline & Personal Comebacks

Health, Habits & Ownership

Replacing bad habits step-by-step, taking radical personal responsibility, and building physical and spiritual endurance.

"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind." — 2 Timothy 1:7
July • Month 5

Digital Wisdom & Healthy Boundaries

Guarding Your Mind

Setting healthy online boundaries, avoiding public reactions during crisis, and choosing quiet, offline healing.

"Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it." — Proverbs 4:23
August • Month 6

Graduation & Community Commissioning

Advocacy & Mentorship

Commemorate your healing journey. Formally receive a diploma or certificate. A final communal commissioning where participants are sent forth as ambassadors for healing.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations... surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age." — Matthew 28:19-20

What to Expect Each Month

Every monthly gathering is structured into an intentional 80-minute interactive format designed for fellowship, learning, and safe table sharing.

15 Minutes

Welcome & Fellowship

Light refreshments, music, and casual connection before the session begins.

25 Minutes

Keynote & Scripture

Teaching on the monthly theme drawing direct lessons from Sudan Fire USA ICE.

30 Minutes

Guided Table Discussions

Small-group reflection, guided journaling, and safe-space sharing with table leaders.

10 Minutes

Call to Action & Prayer

An encouraging monthly challenge and closing prayer for the weeks ahead.

"Survival is not the finish line—becoming a voice for others is."

About the Speaker

Author of "Sudan Fire USA ICE" • Founder of Nuel Property Services

Emmanuel Modi is an author, entrepreneur, and community speaker operating in Central Minnesota. Having overcome displacement, war, mental health battles, and complex life transitions, Emmanuel grounds his speaking ministry in radical personal accountability, steady work ethic, and spiritual faith.

Through the Survive. Heal. Rise. community series, Emmanuel dedicates his story to creating safe spaces where individuals, youth, and families can break free from stigma and step into purpose.
