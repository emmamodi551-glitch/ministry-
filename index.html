The Story Behind the Movement

The Story Behind Survive. Heal. Rise.

From survival to purpose. From trauma to testimony. From silence to a voice.

Survive. Heal. Rise. was born from a journey that began in circumstances a child should never have experienced. Today, that story has become a platform for faith, creativity, community, healing, and purpose.

The movement is about recognizing where we have been without allowing our past to determine where we are going.

The six-month curriculum uses storytelling, devotional reflection, creative expression, and community conversation. The book collection provides additional perspectives that can support different parts of that journey.

The Larger Vision

The Nuel Book Collection

A long-term creative collection built around growth, resilience, faith, art, identity, and legacy.

The collection begins with foundational works such as Sudan Fire USA ICE, God, My Lifeguard, Art Is Love, Unique Poetry, and You Still Running.

These books do not have to function as separate projects. They can also serve as complementary resources within the Survive. Heal. Rise. curriculum, allowing different themes to be explored through memoir, faith, creativity, poetry, perseverance, and personal reflection.

As the collection grows, future books can be incorporated into additional curriculum themes and community conversations without changing the foundation of the six-month series.

  • Identity and personal transformation
  • Faith and spiritual growth
  • Art, poetry, music, and creative expression
  • Resilience and overcoming adversity
  • Community development and empowerment
  • Long-term creative and organizational legacy
Six-Month Journey

Survive. Heal. Rise. Curriculum

Each month addresses a different part of the journey from surviving difficult experiences to healing, rebuilding, and rising into purpose.

The curriculum is designed to grow with the book collection. Sudan Fire USA ICE provides the featured foundational story, while the additional books can be used as complementary resources for specific themes, discussions, creative exercises, and reflection.

Month 1 • March

Reclaiming Your Story

Identity & Overcoming Stigma

Breaking the silence around trauma and difficult experiences while discovering that your past does not have to define your future.

Featured Resource Sudan Fire USA ICE — survival, identity, migration, resilience, and testimony.
"For I know the plans I have for you..." Jeremiah 29:11
Month 2 • April

Resilience Through the Seasons

Faith in Hardship

Learning how faith, patience, and resilience can help us continue moving forward during difficult seasons.

Curriculum Resource God, My Lifeguard — faith, spiritual anchoring, healing, obedience, and perseverance.
"The Lord will guide you always..." Isaiah 58:11
Month 3 • May

The Power of Creative Renewal

Art, Music & Creative Outlets

Discovering how creativity can become an outlet for expression, reflection, connection, and personal renewal.

Curriculum Resource Art Is Love and Unique Poetry — creative expression, emotion, imagination, identity, and finding a voice.
"We are God's handiwork..." Ephesians 2:10
Month 4 • June

Mercy, Compassion & Service

Serving Others

Moving beyond our own struggles to recognize the needs of others and become people who bring mercy, compassion, kindness, and service.

Curriculum Resource Art Is Love — exploring love, connection, empathy, creativity, and the human experience through creative expression.
"Be kind and compassionate to one another..." Ephesians 4:32
Month 5 • July

Strength & Perseverance

Keep Moving Forward

Building the strength to continue through setbacks, uncertainty, disappointment, and obstacles.

Featured Resource You Still Running — perseverance, movement, purpose, struggle, and continuing forward.
"I can do all things through Christ..." Philippians 4:13
Month 6 • August

Restoration & Rising

Healing, Purpose & Renewal

Bringing the journey together by focusing on healing, restoration, purpose, hope, and the courage to rise into the next chapter.

Curriculum Resources The full collection comes together here — Sudan Fire USA ICE, God, My Lifeguard, Art Is Love, Unique Poetry, and You Still Running.
"He heals the brokenhearted..." Psalm 147:3
