The Vision

A Journey of Transformation

Rooted in lived experience, faith, and community resilience.

Survive. Heal. Rise. is more than a series—it is a movement born out of deep resilience. Based in St. Cloud, Minnesota, this initiative offers a safe space for individuals and communities to move past trauma and step into their God-given purpose.

Led by Emmanuel Modi, the program weaves together practical wisdom, storytelling, faith-based guidance, and creative expression to empower every participant.

The Structure

About the 6-Month Series

A comprehensive roadmap designed for steady, profound personal growth.

Each month builds intentionally on the previous one, ensuring that participants don't just process past pain, but actively build a thriving, purpose-driven future.

  • Weekly guided discussions and workshops
  • Interactive community storytelling circles
  • Practical resource sharing and mentorship
