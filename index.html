The Larger Vision

The Nuel Book Collection

A long-term creative collection built around growth, resilience, faith, art, identity, and legacy.

The collection begins with foundational works such as Sudan Fire USA ICE, God, My Lifeguard, Art Is Love, Unique Poetry, and You Still Running.

Future books are intended to expand the collection into broader themes of personal development, creativity, community, faith, business, systems, and institutional legacy.

  • Identity and personal transformation
  • Faith and spiritual growth
  • Art, poetry, music, and creative expression
  • Resilience and overcoming adversity
  • Community development and empowerment
  • Long-term creative and organizational legacy
Six-Month Journey

Survive. Heal. Rise. Curriculum

Each month addresses a different part of the journey from surviving difficult experiences to healing, rebuilding, and rising into purpose.

Month 1 • March

Reclaiming Your Story

Identity & Overcoming Stigma

Breaking the silence around trauma and difficult experiences while discovering that your past does not have to define your future.

"For I know the plans I have for you..." Jeremiah 29:11
Month 2 • April

Resilience Through the Seasons

Faith in Hardship

Learning how faith, patience, and resilience can help us continue moving forward during difficult seasons.

"The Lord will guide you always..." Isaiah 58:11
Month 3 • May

The Power of Creative Renewal

Art, Music & Creative Outlets

Discovering how creativity can become an outlet for expression, reflection, connection, and personal renewal.

"We are God's handiwork..." Ephesians 2:10
Month 4 • June

Mercy, Compassion & Service

Serving Others

Moving beyond our own struggles to recognize the needs of others and become people who bring mercy, compassion, kindness, and service.

"Be kind and compassionate to one another..." Ephesians 4:32
Month 5 • July

Strength & Perseverance

Keep Moving Forward

Building the strength to continue through setbacks, uncertainty, disappointment, and obstacles.

"I can do all things through Christ..." Philippians 4:13
Month 6 • August

Restoration & Rising

Healing, Purpose & Renewal

Bringing the journey together by focusing on healing, restoration, purpose, hope, and the courage to rise into the next chapter.

"He heals the brokenhearted..." Psalm 147:3

Downtown St. Cloud Community Series

Survive. Heal. Rise. is designed as a monthly community gathering connected to the themes of the memoir and the broader mission of encouraging healing, resilience, faith, creativity, and purpose.

  • Downtown St. Cloud, Minnesota
  • Six monthly gatherings from March through August
  • Community-centered discussion and participation
  • Devotional and scripture-based reflection

Interested in Attending?

  • Individuals seeking encouragement
  • Community members
  • Creatives and artists
  • Faith and ministry participants
