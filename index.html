The 6-Month Curriculum Roadmap

Each month focuses on a core theme designed to equip individuals, youth, and families with spiritual anchors, emotional wisdom, and practical habits.

March • Month 1

Reclaiming Your Story

Identity & Overcoming Stigma

Breaking the silence of trauma and difficult life experiences. Discovering that a diagnosis, past failure, or difficult chapter does not define your future.

"For I know the plans I have for you... plans to give you hope and a future." — Jeremiah 29:11
April • Month 2

Resilience Through the Seasons

Faith in Hardship

Learning how to keep your spiritual and emotional anchor steady when circumstances, environments, or seasons of life change.

"The Lord will guide you always; he will satisfy your needs in a sun-scorched land." — Isaiah 58:11
May • Month 3

The Power of Creative Renewal

Art, Music & Outlets

Using visual art, music, writing, and other healthy creative outlets as tools for expression, reflection, growth, and renewal.

"He has put a new song in my mouth, a hymn of praise to our God." — Psalm 40:3
June • Month 4

Discipline & Personal Comebacks

Health, Habits & Ownership

Building better habits step by step, taking personal responsibility, and developing physical, emotional, and spiritual endurance.

"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and a sound mind." — 2 Timothy 1:7
July • Month 5

Digital Wisdom & Healthy Boundaries

Guarding Your Mind

Setting healthy online boundaries, practicing wisdom during difficult moments, and making room for quiet, offline reflection.

"Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it." — Proverbs 4:23
August • Month 6

Graduation & Community Commissioning

Advocacy & Mentorship

Commemorate the journey, celebrate growth, recognize participants, and encourage continued service, mentorship, advocacy, and purpose.

"Therefore go and make disciples of all nations... surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age." — Matthew 28:19-20

What to Expect Each Month

Every monthly gathering is structured into an intentional 80-minute interactive format designed for fellowship, learning, reflection, and safe table sharing.

15 Minutes

Welcome & Fellowship

Light refreshments, music, and casual connection before the session begins.

25 Minutes

Keynote & Scripture

Teaching on the monthly theme drawing lessons from Sudan Fire USA ICE.

30 Minutes

Guided Table Discussions

Small-group reflection, guided journaling, and optional safe-space sharing with table leaders.

10 Minutes

Call to Action & Prayer

An encouraging monthly challenge and closing prayer for the weeks ahead.

"Survival is not the finish line—becoming a voice for others is."

About the Speaker

Author of Sudan Fire USA ICE • Founder of Nuel Property Services

Emmanuel Modi is an author, entrepreneur, and community speaker operating in Central Minnesota. His work draws from personal experiences of displacement, difficult transitions, resilience, faith, accountability, and rebuilding.

Through the Survive. Heal. Rise. community series, Emmanuel seeks to create welcoming spaces where individuals, youth, and families can move beyond stigma, discover purpose, develop healthy habits, and encourage one another.
